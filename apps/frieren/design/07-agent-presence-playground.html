The shoulder avatar

One face, two homes: a small chip inside the pill at rest, and the shoulder when engaged — bigger, half of it above the top border, inset from the left border so nothing protrudes sideways. It travels between the two; it never appears.

Kodama is drawn, so its expressions really change. Portrait and Photo are the real assets with no per-expression art — that mode is what ships today.
